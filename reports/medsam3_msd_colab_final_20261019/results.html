Interpretation

  • Full combination gains range from +3.33 to +8.03 points. Its fold-mean gains are +4.74, +5.71 and +5.71 points.
  • The full combination leads Edge + SDF + Depth by only 0.44 points and wins 7/9 pairs. This smaller ranking difference is less secure than the improvement over baseline.
  • Full-combination FN falls from 869.18 to 555.99 voxels/volume (−36.0%); FP rises from 310.63 to 436.97 (+40.7%); A/P swaps fall from 123.13 to 97.59 (−20.7%). This is a sensitivity/false-positive trade-off.
  • Training Dice rises from 81.00% to 86.88%, alongside validation improvement. The train–validation gap is 4.66 points for baseline and 5.16 for the full combination.
  • SDF alone is weak and variable (+0.62 points, 6/9 positive pairs; negative fold-mean gains in folds 0 and 1). Depth alone improves 8/9 pairs. Every fresh multi-constraint condition improves all 9 pairs.
  • Selected folds and repeated seeds do not establish statistical significance or nine independent test sets. The condition ranking is exploratory, and was selected using these validation results.
