ADNI: 3 folds x 3 seeds, no augmentation

1. Create My Drive/hippo_adni_3fold_3seed/input/ in Google Drive.
2. Upload adni_data_verified.zip and adni_replication_code.zip there. Keep them zipped.
3. Open ADNI_3fold_3seed_no_augmentation.ipynb using Colab > File > Upload notebook.
4. Choose A100 or H100 (native BF16). Run cells in order; required GPU preflight blocks training on failure.
5. Results and resumable checkpoints go to My Drive/hippo_adni_3fold_3seed/results_noaug_v1/.

504 fresh runs = 4 models x 14 original conditions x folds 0/1/2 x seeds 0/1/2.
Each saved fold: 5 training subjects (10 L/R crops), 27 validation subjects (54 crops).
No augmentation or test-time augmentation. Deterministic normalization/padding is unchanged.
nnUNet: native foreground Dice+CE, deep supervision, SGD; 60% targets and 75% p95 ceiling.
SwinUNETR / UNETR / 3D U-Net: original Dice including background, AdamW; 10% targets and 50% p95 ceiling.
Optimization budget: 31,976,450,667,090 FLOPs per run. Calibration counted separately.

Do not replace or interrupt the running MSD notebook. You may upload ADNI while MSD runs;
start ADNI after MSD finishes if reusing its Colab runtime. ADNI uses separate code/data/results paths.

Drive should have at least 8 GiB free; the runtime SSD should have at least 20 GiB free at setup.
Use one notebook at a time for this results folder. Completed metrics are archived and verified
before that run's generated checkpoints are removed. Datasets and source ZIPs remain on Drive.
The notebook uses a 22-hour clean-stop target for Pro+; runtime and compute units are not guaranteed.
To stop cleanly, interrupt the training cell once and wait for checkpoint upload to finish.
Rerun the setup/preflight/run cells on the same GPU/software to continue verified work.
Abrupt loss of a runtime with updates newer than the durable checkpoint requires a recovery audit;
those charged updates are never silently erased or replayed as fresh work.

Local packaging, original loss graph, saved subject split and data checks are recorded beside
these files. The actual GPU preflight is executed in your authenticated Colab runtime.
