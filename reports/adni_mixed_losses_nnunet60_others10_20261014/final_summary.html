ADNI: final 56-run comparison

All 56 experiments completed, archives verified and bulky generated artifacts cleaned. Saved 5% subject split: 5 training subjects / 10 crops; 27 validation subjects / 54 crops. Fold 0, seed 0, no augmentation. Primary results: final optimization-budget checkpoint. Single fold/seed, exploratory; model selection uses these validation results.

nnUNet uses native foreground Dice + CE, deep supervision and SGD, with 60% sequential constraint targets and a 75% p95 ceiling. The other models use MONAI Dice including background and AdamW, with 10% targets and a 50% ceiling. Architecture and baseline-loss/optimizer effects are confounded across models; use matched within-model gains.
