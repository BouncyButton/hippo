How these results were reached: native 96³ volumes, 19 training volumes (saved 5% subset), 96 validation volumes, fixed seeds 0/1/2, full-budget stopping. UNETR needed an explored recipe with peak LR 6×10⁻⁴ and XY D4 augmentation. Other models use no augmentation. The frozen baseline recipe was reused for each matched constraint run.

Constraint-strength exploration led to UNETR 4× (gradient target 0.4, p95 ceiling 2.0) and nnUNet 6× (target 3.6, ceiling 4.5). Swin and 3D U-Net retain target 0.1 / ceiling 0.5. These are gradient-ratio targets, not loss coefficients or compute multipliers. The 182.178-TFLOP cap is identical across models and strengths. nnUNet’s fold-0 Bands+SDF+Depth improved from 72.53% at original strength to 78.72% at 6×; the completed broader sweep then found all four best at 78.22% across folds.

Why constraints help: bands supervise the region around the target boundary; edge terms add boundary detail; SDF encodes signed distance; depth weights supervision using the geometry. Gradient calibration scales auxiliary terms relative to the native objective, with warm-up and a p95 ceiling. The observed improvements support better spatial supervision under limited labels/compute; these experiments do not isolate a universal causal mechanism.

Medical training: nnUNet uses native foreground Dice+CE, deep supervision and SGD (60% auxiliary target, 75% p95 ceiling). SwinUNETR, UNETR and 3D U-Net use Dice and AdamW (10% target, 50% ceiling). All medical sweeps have no augmentation. FLOP-aware schedules give cheaper models more updates, while charging auxiliary computation.

Execution: cached preprocessing, validated GPU acceleration and concurrent independent workers made the sweeps practical. Frozen protocols and initializations preserve matched comparisons; resumable ledgers track interruptions. Evaluations and compact archives are verified before checkpoint cleanup. These execution improvements should be described as throughput/reliability work, not as scientific causes of Dice improvement.

Limits: MSD seed 20 replaced seed 1, ADNI seed 4 replaced seed 2 after inspection. Different model losses, optimizers, strengths, supports, folds and augmentation prevent clean architecture-only comparisons. Fold/seed cells are not nine independent test sets. Report wins descriptively; do not claim significance from these tables. Foundation-model pretraining and fixed-update budgets prevent a fair compute-efficiency ranking against training from scratch.
