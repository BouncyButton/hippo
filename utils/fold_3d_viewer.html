Reading the masks and errors

Extra foreground: the model labels tissue outside the annotation. Missed foreground: annotated tissue is predicted as background. A/P swap: foreground is detected but given the wrong anterior/posterior label.

In “Predicted mask + errors”, correct predictions retain their A/P colors. Error views display the union of annotation and prediction so that missed tissue stays visible. Internal errors may be hidden behind the opaque 3D surface; inspect their coronal slices.

Slice indices refer to the original NIfTI grid (zero-based). MRI uses a fixed per-case 1st–99th percentile window. The mask surface uses exact voxel faces, without smoothing. Connected components use 4-neighbor connectivity within each annotation slice.

A dashed cut appears only when the annotation has an exactly planar A/P split. Checkerboard areas are padding outside the acquired MRI; predicted errors there are retained. No physical left/right hemisphere identity is inferred from these cropped files.
